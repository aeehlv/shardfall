# Kelvarrow — World Overview

**Game:** Shardfall (working title)
**World name:** Kelvarrow

---

## The Shattering

Kelvarrow was born with a heart of light. Deep beneath its crust burned the **Undersun** — a buried crystalline star, older than stone, whose slow radiance seeped upward through veins of quartz and fed every living and unliving thing. The Undersun did not merely shine; it *sang*. Its resonance was the source of all magic. Rivers ran where its verses pooled. Forests grew in the rhythm of its choruses. The first mages were simply people who learned to hum along.

For an age beyond counting, the song held the world together. Then, on a night the calendars refuse to name, the song reached a note no throat was built to survive.

The Undersun burst.

There was no warning — only a single, perfect chord felt in the teeth of every creature alive, and then the floor of the world heaved. Continents tore along the old crystal veins like bread broken along its seams. Mountains folded. The Sunken Sea poured into a chasm that had been a kingdom an hour before. And out of the wound at the world's core rose a slow, glittering storm: the substance of the shattered star itself, flung into the sky and falling back for a full year afterward. Survivors called it the **Year of Falling Light**, when it snowed radiance on the ruins and no night was ever fully dark.

What fell were the **Aethershards**.

Each shard is a fragment of the Undersun — a splinter of frozen songlight, warm to the touch, faintly chiming, luminous forever. Each holds one note of the original worldsong, and a single note, properly struck, is still power beyond reason. A shard the size of a knuckle can light a city for a generation, or level one in an afternoon. Shards do not tarnish, do not dim, and cannot be counterfeited; hold one to your ear and you will hear it singing, and no forgery has ever learned the tune. They are the coin of the mighty and the prize of every war since. Kingdoms have been bought with a pouch of them. Kingdoms have been burned for less.

For the shards are running out of sky. The Year of Falling Light ended long ago; every shard that will ever exist already lies somewhere in the broken world, and most of the easy ones are taken. What remains is contested — dredged from drowned cathedrals, chipped from caldera walls, grown over by roots that refuse to give them up.

Three great powers now war over the scattered heart of the world, and their quarrel is not merely greed. Each believes it knows what the Shattering *meant*. The Pyre Dominion calls it a divine forging, and would gather the shards into fire. The Abyssal Choir calls it the opening of a hymn, and would gather the shards into song. The Verdant Compact calls it a seeding, and would let the shards take root where they fell.

They cannot all be right. On the shardfields of Kelvarrow, that argument is settled the old way — one battle, one shard, at a time.

## Keywords Glossary

Exact rules text for the eight gameplay keywords. Card text should use these definitions verbatim.

| Keyword | Rules Text |
|---|---|
| **Rush** | This unit can attack the turn it is played. |
| **Guard** | Enemies must attack this unit before they can attack your other units or your hero. |
| **Piercing** | When this unit attacks and destroys a unit, excess damage is dealt to the enemy hero. |
| **Lifesteal** | Damage dealt by this card also heals your hero for that much. |
| **Arrival** | Triggers this card's effect when it is played from your hand. |
| **Last Rites** | Triggers this unit's effect when it dies. |
| **Ignite X** | At the end of your turn, deal X damage to a random enemy. |
| **Overgrow** | At the end of each of your turns, if this unit is on the battlefield, it gains +1/+1. |

**Clarifications**

- **Guard:** if multiple friendly units have Guard, the attacker may choose among them.
- **Piercing:** excess damage applies only when attacking a unit, not when defending.
- **Lifesteal:** applies to all damage the card deals, including combat and effect damage.
- **Arrival:** does not trigger when the unit is summoned by another card's effect.
- **Ignite X:** "a random enemy" includes enemy units and the enemy hero.
- **Overgrow:** the bonus is permanent and stacks each turn the unit survives.

## The Three Factions

### Pyre Dominion (slug: `pyre`)

**Colors:** ember red / blackened bronze · **Playstyle:** aggressive tempo, direct damage

In the caldera-lands of the Cinderreach, where the Shattering split the crust down to living magma, the Pyre Dominion built an empire out of the wound itself. To the Dominion, the cataclysm was no tragedy: it was a *forging*. The Undersun was raw ore, they preach, and the Shattering was the hammerblow of a smith too vast to see — the first strike in a work not yet finished. Their sacred duty is to finish it. In the heart of their capital roars the **Great Crucible**, a furnace the size of a city fed by captive lava-veins, into which every recovered Aethershard is cast; when the last shard joins the melt, the world will be reforged whole and burning, and the Dominion will be its edge. They are legion-smiths and slag-knights, magmatic war-beasts in bronze barding, foundry-priests who read scripture in sparks. Their armies move like poured metal — fast, hot, and impossible to hold back — striking before an enemy can set their feet and burning down whatever stands. The Dominion does not besiege. The Dominion *ignites*.

### Abyssal Choir (slug: `abyss`)

**Colors:** teal / violet bioluminescence · **Playstyle:** control, spells, card manipulation

When the Sunken Sea swallowed the old kingdom of the deeps, its people did not all drown — some *changed*, and in the crushing dark they heard what no surface ear can: the Aethershards still singing. Each shard hums its one surviving note of the worldsong, and the Abyssal Choir has spent generations learning to sing back. Their drowned congregation gathers in cathedral-trenches lit by their own violet and teal luminescence, robed clergy with too many eyes arranged in choirs of thirty-three, weaving recovered shards into **shard-hymns** — harmonies that bend reality the way water bends light. A well-sung hymn can unmake a spell mid-cast, rewrite a memory, or call a possibility out of the deep that was never supposed to surface. The Choir believes the Shattering was not an ending but an *opening*: the worldsong's first movement concluded, and its second waits to be performed — but only if every note is gathered and sung in true order. So they collect, and they listen, and they wait. In battle the Choir never hurries. They answer, unravel, and redirect, drowning an enemy's plans one countermeasure at a time until the moment arrives for a single, perfect, annihilating chord.

### Verdant Compact (slug: `verdant`)

**Colors:** living green / warm gold · **Playstyle:** growth, swarm, healing

Where the shards fell on forest, the forest answered. Roots found the buried songlight and coiled around it — and grew, and *woke*. The Verdant Compact is the alliance that rose from that waking: elder forest-spirits with rings of gold light in their bark, druid circles who tend shardfall sites like sacred orchards, and the beast-kin herds and packs who swore the Root-Oath beside them. The Compact holds the one belief the other powers call naive and cannot disprove: the Aethershards are not weapons and not notes — they are **seeds**. The Undersun did not die; it went to seed the way a great tree does at the end of its season, scattering itself so that something new could grow. Melting the shards is murder; hoarding them in the dark is worse. Planted in living soil, a shard sends out roots of light, and the Compact has learned to graft those roots across the broken edges of the world — vast living sutures of trunk and vine that already bind three sundered landmasses. Their armies are the forest walking: green tides of beast-kin that thicken faster than they can be cut down, groves that heal what the war wounds, saplings that stand a little taller every dawn. The Compact grows. That is its whole strategy, and it has never once stopped working.

## Ranked Zones

The ranked ladder is a journey across shattered Kelvarrow, from the shard-dusted fields where every duelist begins to the wound at the heart of the world.

1. **The Glasswake** — Rolling borderlands still glittering with shard-dust from the Year of Falling Light, where every would-be champion fights their first duels for splinters barely worth naming.
2. **The Cinderreach** — The Pyre Dominion's caldera-lands, a maze of bronze roads and lava-veins where the duels turn fast, hot, and unforgiving.
3. **The Sunken Antiphon** — The drowned cathedral-trenches of the Abyssal Choir, where challengers battle beneath bioluminescent vaults and the shards themselves seem to judge the melody of each play.
4. **The Greatgraft** — The Verdant Compact's colossal living suture, a continent-spanning bridge of root and gold-lit heartwood where only disciplined, hardened duelists survive the crossing.
5. **The Heartwound** — The crater where the Undersun burst, ringed by fragments of frozen songlight the size of towers, where the world's finest meet and every victory echoes through the broken worldsong itself.
