# UI Art Prompts — Buttons

Shared rules for all button assets:

- Buttons are generated as **art-only plates**: ornate fantasy metalwork, isolated on a flat near-black background (`#101014`) for easy keying/cropping. Generous empty margin on all sides.
- **No text in the artwork.** Labels ("End Turn", "Cancel", "Concede", …) are rendered in HTML on top. The button face must therefore stay a smooth, low-detail central panel with clear contrast headroom for a label.
- Deliver each button as a horizontal rounded plate, roughly 3:1 face proportions, centered in a 16:9 canvas.
- Style stays on-brand: painterly fantasy, hand-forged look, warm Aethershard radiance as the signature light. No photorealism, no flat vector/sticker style.
- States (hover, pressed, disabled) are handled in-engine via tinting and glow — only one neutral-state image per button is needed.

## 1. Primary Action Button — "End Turn"

**Role:** the single most-clicked element in the game. Must read as warm, inviting, and important. Gold, Aethershard-lit.

**PROMPT:**
A single ornate fantasy game button plate, a wide horizontal rounded slab of polished antique gold with hand-chased scrollwork edges, its smooth empty central face gently domed and softly lit to hold a label, flanked on each end by a small faceted crystal shard cabochon glowing with warm inner radiance, fine engraved filigree confined to the outer rim only, subtle warm light emanating from the seams. Rich saturated gold and amber palette with deep bronze shadows and pale crystalline glints. The object floats centered and isolated on a flat near-black background with generous empty margin on all sides, nothing else in frame. Lit by its own warm crystal glow plus a soft top-down key light, regal and inviting mood. Painterly digital fantasy illustration, rich saturated color, confident visible brushstrokes, dramatic cinematic lighting, AAA collectible card game interface art quality. Wide 16:9 aspect ratio. No text, no lettering, no watermark, no border, no UI elements.
