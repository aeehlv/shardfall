# Shardfall — Ranked Ladder Zones

The ranked climb is a journey across shattered Kelvarrow, from the shard-dusted borderlands where every duelist begins to the wound at the heart of the world. Each rank tier plays out on that zone's battlefield; crossing into a new tier is framed as physically traveling deeper into the broken world.

Battlefield art specs live in this directory, one file per zone (slug filenames). Unlock lists below are sketches — economy and cosmetics teams will refine.

---

## 1. The Glasswake — **Bronze**

Rolling borderlands still glittering with shard-dust from the Year of Falling Light. The grass itself glints, splinters of songlight too small to fight over lie half-buried in the meadows, and every would-be champion fights their first duels here for shards barely worth naming. It is the only zone that feels welcoming — Kelvarrow before the war gets serious.

**Unlocks (sketch):**
- Battlefield: *The Glasswake* board (default, all players).
- Cards: core set completion drip — a starter-deck upgrade card at each Bronze subrank.
- Cosmetics: "Glasswake Prospector" card back; shard-dust victory sparkle emote.

## 2. The Cinderreach — **Silver**

The Pyre Dominion's caldera-lands: a maze of bronze roads, slag battlements, and open lava-veins under a furnace-lit sky, with the Great Crucible glowing on every horizon. Duels here turn fast, hot, and unforgiving — the first real test of tempo and nerve. Silver is where the ladder starts fighting back.

**Unlocks (sketch):**
- Battlefield: *The Cinderreach* board.
- Cards: one guaranteed `pyre` rare on tier entry; Silver subranks award Pyre-slanted packs.
- Cosmetics: "Crucible Bronze" card back; molten End Turn button skin; hero forge-flourish animation.

## 3. The Sunken Antiphon — **Gold**

The drowned cathedral-trenches of the Abyssal Choir, where challengers battle beneath bioluminescent vaults, god-rays falling from a surface too far away to matter. The shards themselves seem to judge the melody of each play, and sloppy lines get unraveled one countermeasure at a time. Gold rewards patience, sequencing, and reading the opponent.

**Unlocks (sketch):**
- Battlefield: *The Sunken Antiphon* board.
- Cards: one guaranteed `abyss` rare on tier entry; Gold subranks award Abyss-slanted packs.
- Cosmetics: "Choir of Thirty-Three" card back; bioluminescent card-glow finisher; drowned-bell rank-up fanfare.

## 4. The Greatgraft — **Diamond**

The Verdant Compact's colossal living suture — a continent-spanning bridge of grafted root and gold-lit heartwood strung across a bottomless break in the world. Only disciplined, hardened duelists survive the crossing, and the ladder population thins to those who intend to reach the top. Diamond is attrition: every mistake compounds, every advantage grows.

**Unlocks (sketch):**
- Battlefield: *The Greatgraft* board.
- Cards: one guaranteed `verdant` epic on tier entry; Diamond subranks award wildcard-style crafting materials.
- Cosmetics: "Root-Oath" card back; golden overgrowth board flourish; animated golden card frames for hero portraits.

## 5. The Heartwound — **Legend**

The crater where the Undersun burst, ringed by fragments of frozen songlight the size of towers. The world's finest meet here, and every victory audibly echoes through the broken worldsong itself. There is nowhere deeper to go — only ranks among the best.

**Unlocks (sketch):**
- Battlefield: *The Heartwound* board (Legend-exclusive; usable in all modes once earned).
- Cards: seasonal Legend card back plus a full-art alternate of the season's marquee legendary.
- Cosmetics: "Heartwound" animated card back; worldsong victory theme; permanent Legend gem on profile; end-of-season leaderboard titles.
