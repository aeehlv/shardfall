# UI Art Prompts — Card Frame Overlays

How frames work in Shardfall (PROVEN RECIPE — this produced the three shipped faction frames):

- A frame is an **overlay asset** composited on top of the 3:4 card illustration. Name, rules text, and stat numbers are rendered in HTML/engine on top of the frame — **never in the artwork**.
- **Canonical layout** (identical on every frame; the shipped frames measure exactly this):
  - **Art window**: ~5%–60% of card height, interior flat solid pure black (keyed out later).
  - **Name plate**: ~61%–71% of card height, ~60% of card width, holds two lines of text.
  - **Rules panel**: ~73%–96% of card height, interior clean plain dark leather — no ornaments
    intruding where text goes.
  - **Four round gem sockets**, one per corner: mana ↖, rarity ↗, attack ↙, health ↘
    (gems render in HTML on top — see `lib/cards.ts` for per-frame socket coordinates).
- Generation pipeline for any new frame:
  1. Generate with `recraft/recraft-v4.1`, size `1024x1365`, using the shared layout clause below
     plus a faction/rarity material description.
  2. Flood-fill the window black from the center to transparency (knockout), export 768w PNG to
     `public/cards/frames/`.
  3. Measure the window bbox and plate interior; add the card's layout coords to `lib/cards.ts`
     and pre-crop the art to the measured window ratio (see `world/style-guide.md`).
  4. If the generated geometry is off, do NOT fight the model with re-prompts — fix it
     deterministically with pixel band-shifts (move the plate band up/down, fill vacated rows by
     copying the rows above), as was done for the shipped frames.
- One neutral base + 3 faction accent variants + 4 rarity variants. Faction and rarity combine in-engine (faction recolors the filigree, rarity swaps the crest gem), so each axis is generated independently against the neutral base.

**Shared layout clause (used verbatim inside every prompt below):**
"a complete ornate fantasy trading card frame overlay in portrait orientation, with the design filling the entire canvas edge-to-edge, with a large empty portrait art window from about 5 to 60 percent of the card height, its interior flat solid pure black; a slim ornate horizontal name plate spanning about 60 percent of the card width and sitting between about 61 and 71 percent of the card height, tall enough for two lines of text, its interior a clean smooth dark surface; a large recessed rules-text panel from about 73 to 96 percent of the card height, its interior a clean plain dark leather surface with no ornaments intruding into it; and one empty round gem socket embedded at each of the four corners of the border"
