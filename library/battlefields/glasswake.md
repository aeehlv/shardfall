# Battlefield Spec — The Glasswake (Zone 1 · Bronze)

**Slug:** `glasswake` · **Ladder tier:** Bronze (starter battlefield, default for all players)
**Canon:** rolling borderlands still glittering with shard-dust from the Year of Falling Light; the proving ground where every duelist begins.

---

## Visual Description

A sunlit stretch of rolling borderland meadow seen from a slightly elevated dueling ground. The land is green and gentle — the one corner of Kelvarrow the Shattering only grazed — but everything glitters: the grass carries a permanent dusting of crystalline shard-powder, and slow motes of radiance still drift down out of the sky like the last snow of the Year of Falling Light.

- **Upper third (vista):** bright morning sky streaked with a faint auroral shimmer; a horizon of softly fractured hills where old quartz veins catch the sun; one distant standing-stone-sized Aethershard splinter glowing warm on a hilltop — the landmark beginners walk toward.
- **Middle band (play area):** calm, even-valued rolling meadow. Low detail, low contrast, no bright shapes. This is the stage the card rows sit on.
- **Lower edge (foreground framing):** weathered stones of an old dueling ring anchoring the bottom corners; tufts of glinting grass and crystal-dusted wildflowers; a half-buried knuckle-sized Aethershard chiming softly; a prospector's abandoned wooden sieve leaning against a stone.

Faction-neutral palette: fresh living greens, warm gold sunlight, pale crystalline sparkle. The recurring light source is warm Aethershard radiance, per the style guide.

## Mood

Hopeful, bright, early-morning. The calm before a long journey — Kelvarrow before the war gets serious. Where every other zone escalates pressure, the Glasswake should feel like an invitation: safe enough to learn, magical enough to promise more. Ambient audio direction: light wind through grass, faint distant chiming (shard-dust resonating), occasional lark-like birdsong with a crystalline overtone.

## Interactive Hotspots

Hearthstone-style clickable scenery. All effects are short (under 2 s), non-blocking, and cosmetic only.

1. **Half-buried Aethershard (foreground, left corner):** click to make it chime a single pure note and pulse warm light; rapid clicks play an ascending three-note motif — a fragment of the worldsong.
2. **Prospector's sieve (foreground, right corner):** click to rattle it; a puff of glittering shard-dust shakes loose and drifts across the lower edge.
3. **Glass-winged moths (midground grass, edge of play area):** click to startle a small flock into the air; their wings scatter prismatic glints before they resettle.
4. **Dueling-ring stone (bottom corner):** click to topple the top stone of a small cairn; it rocks, rolls, and magically restacks itself with a soft hum.
5. **Distant hilltop shard (upper third):** click to make it flare gently and send a slow ripple of light along the quartz vein running down the hill — a long-cooldown "showpiece" hotspot.
6. **Crystal-dusted dandelion clocks (foreground):** click to puff a seedhead apart; the seeds drift upward, each one glinting like a micro-shard.
7. **Auroral shimmer (sky):** click to make the shimmer briefly brighten and drop three or four extra motes of falling light.
